Thrips and mites (leaf curl complex): Upward curling of leaves indicates thrips;
downward curling indicates yellow mites. Both flare in hot, dry spells. Install
blue sticky traps for thrips. Spray neem seed kernel extract 5 percent as a first
response. Avoid indiscriminate insecticide use which kills natural enemies and
worsens mite outbreaks. Consult KVK before acaricide application.

Anthracnose (fruit rot, Colletotrichum): Warm humid weather with intermittent rain
favours fruit rot. Circular sunken lesions with concentric rings on ripening fruit
are diagnostic. Remove and destroy infected fruit. Ensure spacing for airflow.
Seed treatment with Trichoderma at nursery stage reduces incidence.

Water and soil: Chilli is shallow-rooted; irrigate frequently but lightly. Avoid
water stress at flowering — it causes flower drop. Optimal soil pH 6.0-7.0. Apply
well-decomposed farmyard manure 25 tonnes per hectare at land preparation.

Regenerative practices: Mulch with paddy straw to conserve moisture and suppress
weeds. Border crop of maize reduces thrips migration.
