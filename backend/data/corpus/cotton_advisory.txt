Pink bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella): Install pheromone traps at 5 per hectare
from square formation stage. Economic threshold: 8 moths per trap per night for 3
consecutive nights, or 10 percent damaged flowers (rosette flowers). Remove and
destroy rosette flowers. Terminate the crop by January to break the life cycle.
Do not extend the crop season; avoid ratoon cotton.

Whitefly and sucking pests: Dry spells with high temperature favour whitefly.
Use yellow sticky traps at 10-12 per hectare. Conserve natural enemies; avoid
early-season synthetic pyrethroid sprays which flare whitefly. Neem oil 5 ml per
litre with 0.5 ml sticker can suppress early populations.

Water and soil: Cotton is sensitive to waterlogging; ensure drainage after heavy
rain events of more than 60 mm. In sandy soils (sand fraction above 50 percent),
split potassium application. Boron deficiency causes square shedding in soils with
pH above 8; confirm with soil test before applying borax.

Regenerative practices: Intercrop with green gram or cowpea (2:1) to fix nitrogen
and reduce pest load. Grow border rows of maize or sorghum as barrier crops against
whitefly migration.
