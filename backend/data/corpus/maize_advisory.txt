Fall armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda): Scout twice a week from seedling to tassel
stage. Look for window-pane feeding on young leaves and ragged holes with sawdust-
like frass in whorls. Threshold: 5 percent damaged plants at seedling stage or 10
percent whorl damage at mid-whorl stage. Apply dry sand or soil mixed with wood ash
into whorls as a first response. Release Trichogramma egg parasitoids where
available. Chemical control only per KVK recommendation; rotate modes of action.

Water: Critical irrigation stages are knee-high, tasseling and grain filling.
Moisture stress at tasseling causes the largest yield loss. With forecast rain
above 40 mm in the coming week, delay irrigation and ensure drainage; maize does
not tolerate standing water for more than 24 hours.

Soil and nutrition: Target soil pH 5.8-7.0. In acidic soils apply lime based on
soil test. Apply nitrogen in 3 splits; deficiency shows as V-shaped yellowing from
leaf tip along the midrib on older leaves.

Regenerative practices: Rotate with legumes (pigeon pea, groundnut). Retain crop
residue as mulch to conserve moisture and build soil organic carbon. Avoid burning
stover.
